veloom is the layer where WordPress content goes omnichannel — written once, veloomed to every app, storefront, and screen it needs to reach.

No rebuilds. No bolted-on APIs. No content stuck behind a single screen it was never built for.

---

## The problem

WordPress powers over 40 % of the internet. But the moment your content needs to reach beyond a single website — a mobile app, a storefront, a voice interface, a digital display — the default REST API becomes the bottleneck. It was built for the Classic Editor era. It returns raw HTML. It forces teams into weeks of custom adapters and brittle glue code, just to get content into the shape a modern frontend actually needs.

Every new surface means another rebuild. Another API. Another thing to maintain.

---

## The vision

Content should be written once and reach everywhere it needs to go — without rebuilding for every new surface, without retraining editors, without dismantling what already works.

**We free WordPress to reach what's next.**

---

## Built for teams done rebuilding

veloom turns WordPress into a fully capable headless content platform. Your editors keep working exactly as they always have. Your developers get clean, typed, component-ready content delivered to any frontend — Next.js, Nuxt, Astro, SvelteKit, a native app, a storefront, wherever.

One plugin. Every surface.
